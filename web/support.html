Support

How can we help?

Most things can be sorted from inside the app. If you're stuck, or something looks wrong, send us a message and we'll get back to you.

Get in touch

Bugs, questions, privacy requests, or reporting something urgent. We reply within a few days, and within 24 hours for reports about content. Open the contact form

Joining a closet

Ask whoever runs the closet to send you its invite code, then open the app, tap the + on the Closets tab, and choose "Join with invite code." Codes aren't case sensitive and the dash is optional — paste it however you received it.

To invite someone to your own closet, open it, tap the members line under the title, and choose Share Invite.

Sharing items

When you add a piece you choose which closets it goes into. Anything you don't share stays in My Clothes, where only you can see it.

To change that later, open the item, tap "···" and choose Manage Sharing. Turning a closet off removes the item from it. Note that leaving a closet does not pull back items you already shared into it — remove them first if you want them gone.

Reporting & blocking

Open any item that isn't yours, tap "···", and choose Report or Block. Blocking is immediate and mutual: you won't see their items and they won't see yours. Reports come straight to us and we act on them within 24 hours.

Deleting your account

Go to the Profile tab and tap Delete Account at the bottom. This removes your profile, your items and their photos, and your closet memberships. It can't be undone.

Closets you created stay with their remaining members after you go. If you want a closet gone entirely, remove your items and ask the others to leave, or get in touch.
