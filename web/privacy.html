Privacy policy

Group Closet is a private wardrobe you share with a handful of people you choose. This page explains, in plain language, what the app stores, who can see it, and how to get rid of it.

What we collect

Only what the app needs to work:

Account You sign in with Apple. We receive an identifier for your account and the name you choose to share. We don't ask Apple for your email address, and we never see your Apple password.
Profile Your display name, an optional photo, and the usual sizes you choose to enter.
Items The photos you take or pick, plus the details you add — name, category, size, colour, occasion tags.
Closets Closet names, membership, invite codes, and which of your items you shared where.
Reports and blocks If you report an item or block someone, we store that so we can act on it and keep the block in effect.

We don't collect location, contacts, health data, or advertising identifiers, and the app contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. If you have opted in to sharing diagnostics with developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may send us anonymised crash reports; we never receive those unless you have chosen to share them.

How we use it

To show your wardrobe to you and to the closets you shared it with, to keep you signed in, to act on reports and blocks, and to respond when you contact support. That's the whole list — we don't profile you, and we don't sell or rent your data to anyone.

Who can see your closet

An item is visible to the members of the closets you shared it with, alongside your name and photo so they know whose it is. Items you keep in My Clothes are visible only to you. There is no public profile and no way to browse strangers' wardrobes.

Anyone with a closet's invite code can join that closet, so share codes only with people you trust. You can remove an item from a closet at any time from that item's sharing options, block a member, or leave a closet entirely.

Please note: leaving a closet removes you from it, but items you had already shared into that closet stay visible to its members. If you want them gone, remove them from the closet — or delete them — before you leave.

Our only data processors are Supabase, which hosts our database and photo storage, and Apple, which handles sign-in. Neither uses your content for their own purposes. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

Storage & security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Photos live in private storage and are delivered through short-lived signed links, so an image URL can't be passed around or indexed indefinitely. Database rules enforce closet membership on every request, not just in the app's interface. Data is stored on servers in Canada.

Keeping & deleting data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Deleting an item removes the item and its photo. Deleting your account from the Profile tab removes your profile, your items, their photos, and your closet memberships.

Two things to know about deletion. Closets you created are not deleted along with your account — they remain for their other members, without you. And copies of deleted data may persist briefly in encrypted database backups before those backups are cycled out.

Your choices

You can edit or delete any item, change or clear your profile details, leave any closet, block a member, or delete your account outright — all from inside the app, without contacting us.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or object to how it's handled. Get in touch through our contact form and we'll respond within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for exercising those rights.

Children

Group Closet isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes

If we make a material change we'll update the date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
