Reporting & enforcement

Every item in a closet you don't own has a report option in its "···" menu, and you can block the person who posted it. Blocking hides that person's items from you and yours from them, immediately and in both directions.

We review reports and act on them within 24 hours. Content that breaks these terms is removed, and the account that posted it may be suspended or terminated without notice. Users who are repeatedly reported, or who post seriously harmful content even once, are ejected from the service.
